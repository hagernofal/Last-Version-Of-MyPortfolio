About

A quick rundown of where I come from and what I'm doing now.

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Zagazig University (2025), graduating Excellent with Honors. I care about writing clean, scalable code and closing the gap between a good frontend experience and the backend logic that supports it.

For my graduation project, I worked on Mobeen, a mobile app that simulates speech-therapy exercises — I handled data collection and preparation for the ML models and helped integrate the ML pipeline into the app.

I'm currently a fellow in ITI's 9-month program (Open Source Applications Development track, Zagazig branch), training on advanced web architectures, backend APIs, and modern frontend frameworks. Outside of coursework, I've competed with ICPC Zagazig for two years and spent two years mentoring beginner programmers as a technical instructor.
